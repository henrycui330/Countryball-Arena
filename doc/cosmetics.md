# Cosmetics — Hats

Countryballs → pick a ball → **Hat** row.

Hats are unlocked via **Gacha** (see `doc/gacha.md`). You can only equip hats you own. “None” is always available.

## Caps
| Hat | Notes |
|-----|--------|
| None | Default |
| Officer Cap | Allied service cap (flipped) |
| Straw Hat | Wide brim |

## Mini countryballs (on your head)
| Hat |
|-----|
| Little USA |
| Little Japan |
| Little Russia |
| Little Kazakhstan |
| Little Belarus |
| Little Ukraine |

Equip is saved per countryball (`cosmetics.hatId`) and syncs to the cloud. Ownership lives in `roster.inventory.hats[]`.

Hats show on the Countryballs portrait and on **you** in the arena.
